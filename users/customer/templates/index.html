FOR CUSTOMERS

Sending and tracking requests

How do I send a print request?
  1. Create a customer account or log in to PrintWise.
  2. Open the Printshops page and choose the printshop you want.
  3. Open the shop details and press Request a print.
  4. Choose an available service, then complete the required options such as paper size, print color, finish, and quantity.
  5. Upload the files you want printed. You may add multiple files and include extra instructions when needed.
  6. Review the information and submit the request. The printshop will receive it and set the final price after reviewing the files and specifications.
Can I track my print request?

Yes. Open My Requests after logging in. You can see whether a request is pending, accepted, printing, ready for pickup, or completed. The page also shows the confirmed price and payment status. Click a request row to review its specifications and uploaded files.

How can I pay?

After the printshop confirms the price, you may pay online by opening the request, choosing Pay online, and scanning one of the printshop's available QR codes. Upload a clear screenshot of the payment receipt so the printshop can verify it. You may also pay directly at the physical printshop when you visit or collect your order.
